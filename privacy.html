Pack Stack privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

This policy covers the Pack Stack app for Android (from Google Play) and the Pack Stack web page.

The short version

The game keeps your progress on your phone and nowhere else. We run no servers and no accounts, so we never receive your name, email, contacts, photos or location. The app shows ads through Google AdMob and takes payments through Google Play. Those two Google services do handle some data, as described below.

What the game stores on your phone

Your progress (robots, stars, coins, settings and the player name you pick), three backup copies of it, the list of offers you were shown, and receipts (purchase tokens) for things you bought so they are never handed over twice. All of it stays on your phone. Erase everything in Settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes it.

Ads

Ads are shown by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (which shows a rough location), device and app details, and how you interact with ads. Where the law asks for it, you are asked for your choice before any ad is loaded, and you can change it later under Settings, Privacy, Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or account details. The game only receives a purchase token that says the payment went through.

Play stats

Only if you say yes: simple counts of what happens in the game (levels started, won and lost, packs opened, offers shown, session length). They never leave your phone.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Purchases sit behind Google Play's own parental controls.

Your choices

Switch ads and ad offers off or on in Settings. Switch play stats off. Change your ad privacy choices. Export your save as a code. Erase everything in Settings at any time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears here and on the game's web page, with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: write to support@example.com.

The web page version

The web page version of Pack Stack shows no real ads and sells nothing. It keeps your save in your browser's storage on your device and sends it nowhere.

How we use your data, in short

On your phone only. Save file, three rolling backups of it, your settings, the list of offers you were shown, purchase receipts and (only with your OK) play stats. All of it is erased by Erase everything in Settings.

Never collected by us. Real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, precise location. We have no server to send them to.

Collected by Google for ads. Advertising ID, IP address and rough location, device and app details, ad views and taps. See the Privacy Policy for your choices.

Collected by Google for purchases. What you bought and your payment details, inside Google Play. The game only sees a purchase token.

Problem reports. A report you write is saved on your phone with the game version, the last few game events and basic device details (screen size and browser engine). It is only shared if you copy it and send it to us yourself.
