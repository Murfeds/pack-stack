Pack Stack privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Pack Stack is a puzzle game. This page explains what it stores and what it does not.

What is stored

  • Your save (levels, stars, coins, robot cards, settings, the player name you type) and up to three backups of it. These stay on your phone, inside the game's own storage. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Play stats, only if you say yes when asked (you can switch this off at any time in Settings, Privacy, which also deletes them). They record things like "level started" or "level won" and also stay on your phone.
  • Problem reports you choose to write in the game are kept on your phone so you can copy and send them yourself.

What is not collected

No account, no email address, no contacts, no location, no advertising ID, no photos. The game has no ads and no purchases at the moment. If that changes, this page will be updated first.

Internet use

The game works with no internet. When it is online it may download a small settings file (live.json, for events and seasons) and, on the web version, game updates and fonts. Like any website, GitHub (which hosts these files) and Google Fonts can see the internet address that asks for them. The game sends none of your data with these requests.

Deleting your data

In the game: Settings, Erase everything. Or uninstall the app, which deletes everything it stored.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Murfeds/pack-stack/issues.
