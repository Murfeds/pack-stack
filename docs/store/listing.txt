APP NAME (30 max)
Pack Stack: Robot Block Puzzle

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 max)
Stack gem blocks, clear lines and collect robot cards with real powers.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 max)
Pack Stack is a cozy block puzzle with a twist: every pack you open adds a robot to your crew, and every robot has a power.

DROP, CLEAR, COLLECT
• Drag gem blocks onto the 8x8 board and clear full rows and columns.
• Win stars and coins, then rip open packs of robot cards.
• Put three robots in your crew: smash a block, zap a row, swap your pieces and more.
• 28 robots to collect, from Common to Legend.

600 LEVELS, 40 WORLDS
• Goals that keep changing: break ice and stone, collect items, build streaks, survive the junk.
• 60 boss levels with rules of their own: gravity, mirrors, fog, giant blocks.
• Three stars to earn on every level.

ENDLESS MODE
• Chase your best score. Boost tiles drop in, ice and rock creep up, and from 20,000 points the jammer blocks arrive.

MADE TO BE FAIR
• Every robot can be found just by playing.
• Pack odds are shown in the game, right above the prices.
• Rewarded ads are always your choice.
• The puzzles work offline.

MORE TO DO
• A daily challenge, a daily chest and a weekly event with a special robot.
• Achievements and seasons.
• Four music styles, a left-handed layout and a reduced-motion option.

Pack Stack contains ads and optional in-app purchases (packs of random robot cards, and a Continue for Endless mode).
